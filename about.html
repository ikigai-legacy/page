𝑨𝒃𝒐𝒖𝒕 𝒖𝒔
The squad founded by nabi and keign in a game called BUD on July 10, 2022, and continued it in Eggy Party from November 2023 to the present.
The family name inspiration comes from the word "Ikigai," a Japanese term that blends two words: "iki," meaning "to live," and "gai," meaning "reason," which translates to "a reason to live." It's a concept that encourages people to discover what truly matters to them and to live a life filled with purpose and joy.
